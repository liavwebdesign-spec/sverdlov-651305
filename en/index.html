02The firm's flagship service

Land Plot Purchase Due Diligence

Before you buy a plot of land, you should know exactly what you're buying. Advocate Leon Sverdlov, with over 10 years of experience in the land sector, gives clients a comprehensive due-diligence report: review of the transaction documents, planning and registration restrictions, ownership and encumbrances, future planning potential, and the legal and financial stability of the selling company. That way, you can make an informed decision before signing, not after.
