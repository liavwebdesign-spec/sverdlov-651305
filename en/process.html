What to bring

Any document related to the matter

A contract or a draft, a Land Registry extract, a police report, medical documents, a will, correspondence. A photo on your phone helps too.

A short timeline

What happened, when, and who the parties are. Two lines are enough, and there is no need to prepare a document.

And if you have nothing

That is perfectly fine. We start from the story and collect the documents together.
