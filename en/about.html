Leon Sverdlov, Advocate

Leon represents private and business clients in real estate transactions and legal proceedings, from the first document to the end of the matter. He runs the files himself, and works in Hebrew, Russian and English. The office is located in Yehud and serves clients throughout the country.

Most enquiries arrive at the stage where things can still be influenced: before a contract is signed, in the first days after an accident, or while a dispute has not yet reached court. That is also the stage at which legal support is worth the most.

Three things worth knowing

In a language you are comfortable with

Hebrew, Russian or English throughout, including the explanations and the documents. No need for an interpreter or for a family member to translate.

Checking beforehand, not fixing afterwards

In a real estate transaction most problems can be prevented. After signing, the room to manoeuvre is considerably smaller.

You will also be told when there is nothing to do

If there is no basis for a claim, or the risk is not worth the proceeding, you will hear that in the first conversation and not six months later.

Prefer to talk?

You can call directly, and if we do not answer we will call you back.
